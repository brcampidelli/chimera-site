The development of AI agents is increasingly influenced by public sentiment and government policies. A recent poll indicates that voters are generally skeptical of AI and data centers, though these issues are not their top priority [[1]](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/995917/data-center-nyt-midterm-poll-september). This skepticism suggests that developers must consider public perception when designing and deploying AI systems, ensuring transparency and ethical considerations are at the forefront.

## Public Perception and Its Implications

The poll reveals a nuanced public attitude toward AI. While there is a leaning against AI and data centers, the lack of strong partisan alignment means that developers cannot rely on political trends to predict future regulations or public acceptance. Instead, they must proactively address concerns related to privacy, security, and the societal impact of AI technologies. This requires a focus on building trust through clear communication and demonstrable benefits.

## Policy Incentives and Their Opportunities

On the policy front, new incentives are emerging that could reshape the AI landscape. For instance, Brazil has introduced the Redata regime, which offers tax suspensions for data centers while mandating commitments to national capacity, research, clean energy, and water efficiency [[3]](https://iabrasilnoticias.com.br/governo-sancionou-o-redata-garantindo-incentivos-a-data-centers-no-brasil/). Such policies create opportunities for developers to align their projects with broader societal goals, potentially gaining both financial and reputational benefits.

## Practical Steps for Developers

For those building AI agents, these developments underscore the importance of integrating ethical considerations and policy compliance into their workflows. Developers should:

1. **Engage with Public Concerns**: Regularly assess public sentiment and incorporate feedback into the design and deployment of AI systems.
2. **Leverage Policy Incentives**: Stay informed about new regulations and incentives that could support their projects, such as those offered by the Redata regime.
3. **Focus on Sustainability**: Align AI development with environmental and societal goals, such as clean energy and efficient resource use, to meet both public and policy expectations.

By addressing these factors, developers can navigate the complex interplay of public sentiment and policy shifts, ensuring their AI agents are both impactful and accepted.
